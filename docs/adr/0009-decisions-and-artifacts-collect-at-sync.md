# Decisions and artifacts collect at sync: three decision sources, research notes the only artifact, map gists never parsed

Status: accepted

Work item: GH-49

Ticket #49 asked how Decision and Artifact records are modeled and sourced, what linkage they require, and what stays out of reach. The decision: `pnpm sync` collects two new top-level snapshot arrays, `decisions[]` and `artifacts[]`, from exactly three decision sources — `adr` (`docs/adr/` files), `resolution` (the closing resolution comment on a decision ticket), and `spec` (one bundle record per spec issue's Implementation-Decisions section, never parsed per-bullet; graduation to an ADR is the escape hatch for a decision that outgrows the section). A conclusion existing as both a resolution and an ADR — every ADR-backed map decision — stays two records grouped by their shared work item, never merged: the ADR is the durable form, the resolution the conversation record. Artifacts are research notes only in v1 (`docs/research/*.md`, kind `research-note`); handoff docs (OS temp dir), questionnaires, wizard scripts, and prototype branches are explicitly not modeled — a prototype's durable output is the verdict on the issue, not the throwaway branch. `DecisionRecord` is `{id, source, workItemId, title, statement, status, supersedes, decidedAt, sourceRef}`: the id is the ADR number (`ADR-0007`) or the work item's own id (`GH-49`); `statement` carries the full resolution comment uncapped — a resolution is the only copy of that content in the snapshot, and ADR 0008 already killed the truncated-copy pattern — and is null for adr and spec sources; `status` (`proposed|accepted|deprecated|superseded`), `supersedes` (an optional `Supersedes: ADR-NNNN` line), and `decidedAt` (issue-derived timestamp) are null wherever the source doesn't declare them. `ArtifactRecord` is `{id: RN-<filename-slug>, kind: research-note, path, title, workItemId}`. Linkage is intrinsic for resolution and spec records and declared for the rest as a `Work item: GH-NN` line — the qualified-id grammar blocker edges use — nullable with a non-blocking warning when absent, never a failed sync (the two-workflow-labels precedent); the ADRs predating the collector are retrofitted with the lines their known work items support in the landing change, and artifact linkage is optional and silent. The ADR collector takes every `docs/adr/NNNN-slug.md` at the top level, parses the H1 as title and the `Status:` line; a missing or unparsable status loads with a sync warning — ADR 0008's fail-closed is gate semantics, this is display data. Prose `#NN` references and the map's Decisions-so-far gists are never parsed: the view rebuilds the index by grouping Decision records on `workItemId`.

## Considered options

- **A hand-maintained decisions registry file** — rejected: pins freshness to whoever remembers to edit it; ADR 0006 made sync the collector for exactly this reason.
- **View-layer joins without first-class records** — rejected: every consumer re-implements source parsing; the seam's job is normalized records.
- **Merging a resolution with its ADR into one record** — rejected: lossy dedupe; grouping by work item keeps both forms visible.
- **Per-bullet parsing of spec Implementation-Decisions sections** — rejected: normalizeStatus-style heuristic drift over free-form prose.
- **Scraping ticket references out of ADR prose** — rejected: "Ticket #46 asked…" is narrative, not a declaration; declared lines are the standing ruling (ADR 0008).
- **Modeling prototype branches, questionnaires, wizard scripts, or handoff docs** — rejected: ephemeral, unscannable (no branch naming convention), or unreachable; modeling them buys a hygiene problem for content the sources don't durably record.
- **Parsing the map's Decisions-so-far gists** — rejected: a third copy of every decision with its own drift; the map is an index, not a store.
- **Fail-closed collection on a missing ADR Status** — rejected: that posture is for gate semantics (the frontier); a missing status is untidy display data — warn and load.
- **Bundling the planning-vocabulary consolidation here** — rejected: retiring legacy `Status:` lines and stored `TicketStatus` is a `TicketRecord` migration, semantically one change with ADR 0008's landing; the fog item re-attaches there (ADR 0007 pointed it at this ticket).

## Consequences

- `types.ts` + `schema.ts` gain `DecisionRecord`/`ArtifactRecord` under the excess-property rejection matrix; sync gains three collectors — the ADR walk, the research-note walk, and map sub-issue enumeration with targeted reads of closed decision tickets' resolution comments (never a sweep, maxPages-capped), all failing soft to empty arrays with a `sources` entry on host repos that lack the conventions.
- Lands together with ADR 0008's richer Map records (`{mapId, ticketIds[], title, url}`) because the decision collector needs the sub-issue machinery; edges and the `dependencies` deletion stay with #51's change — two independent migrations over the same files.
- The ADRs with a known producing work item gain `Work item: GH-NN` lines in the same change (0002 via #7; 0005–0009 via their map decision tickets #44–#47 and #49); ADRs 0001, 0003, and 0004 predate ticket-linked decisions and stay unlinked — their missing linkage warns per the declared-line rule on every sync. Research notes carry the line only where a producing ticket is known.
- Unblocks #53's decisions/ADR-history view data and gives #52 the records; the map-rendering feature renders decisions from records instead of body scraping.
- Freshness is as-of-last-successful-sync throughout: resolution comments are fixed at close, ADR files at edit, spec bundles re-collect live each sync. Spec bundles source from the issues the tracker adapter already reads — the open sweep, map membership, targeted reads — never a closed-history sweep, so a spec issue that closes outside a map retires its bundle; if the decisions view ever needs closed-spec bundles, that is a bounded-label-query decision of its own.
- ADR 0007's "(with #49)" vocabulary consolidation moves to ADR 0008's landing change, recorded here so the pointer has an anchor.
